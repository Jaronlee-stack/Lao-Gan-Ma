## Direction contract

THESIS: Lao Gan Ma is a chili-sauce counter kitchen. The user is a diner seated at the pass; their project idea is an order written by hand on a ticket. Press submit and the chef pins the ticket to the rail and cooks the prompt + skills.md, then plates and serves it. Refuses the generic SaaS card-stack-on-slate arrangement: no flat dashboard, no four-color button soup, no emoji standing in for icons. The planner is a scene with depth, not a form.

OWN-WORLD: A warm wooden counter under low kitchen light. Deep woodgrain pass (walnut/oak browns) in the foreground, a tiled splashback wall behind, a lit order rail (ticket wire) across the top. Chili-red (#c42a20/#e23b2e) is the house accent — the sauce, the chef's apron trim, the ticket stamp. Paper tickets are cream (#fbf7 ee) with a torn top edge and a red margin line; typed text appears in a self-hosted handwriting face (Caveat), ink settling char by char. The chef is an authored SVG character (white toque, warm skin, chili-red apron) with pose states. Real depth: CSS perspective + preserve-3d, layered planes, soft contact shadows, drifting steam. Drawn SVG icon system, one stroke weight. Dark mode = dinner service (dimmed counter); light = lunch service.

STORY: The diner understands instantly this is a kitchen that takes their order and cooks it. They write their idea on the ticket (it reads like real handwriting), hand it over, watch the chef work (steam, sizzle, the ticket on the rail), and receive a finished dish: the one-shot prompt and skills.md plated and ready to copy. Phases are courses they can send back (reject) or question (discuss). Approvals are the chef checking before a consequential step.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A countertop scene at full bleed. Background: tiled kitchen wall with the order rail wire strung across the top. Midground center: a cream order ticket lying on the counter at a slight tilt, pinned corner, red margin line, the project-idea textarea living inside it as handwriting. Lower right of the ticket: a chili-stamped "Send to kitchen" primary action (the submit). Left or behind the pass: the chef character, idle, waiting. Foreground: the near edge of the wooden counter in sharp woodgrain, catching warm light. The whole thing sits in perspective so the counter recedes. Primary action (submit the ticket) is the brightest, most forward element.

FORM: A pass-line counter kitchen / order-ticket rail. Chosen by the user (brief-pinned), beats the roll. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
